Classifying Polynomials and Finding Degree

A monomial has exactly one term, a binomial two, a trinomial three. All of them are polynomials; with 4 or more terms we just say “polynomial”.
9y · x-5 · x^2+4x-1
The degree of a monomial is the sum of the exponents on its variables. The degree of a polynomial is the largest degree among its terms.
m^4n^2: 4+2=6 · a constant like -3 has degree 0
A variable with a negative exponent (like x^{-2}=\tfrac{1}{x^2}) is not allowed in a polynomial term, so an expression containing one is not a polynomial.
!
Two common slips. The highest-degree term isn’t always written first (4x^6-3x^8 has degree 8). And a term with two variables adds both exponents (4x^2y^4 has degree 6, not 4).
Example 1

A8x^3
Bx-5
C-3
D9y

(b) Find the degree of the polynomial m^4n^2+5p^3.
Record your answer left to right.
m^4n^2: 4+2=6; 5p^3: 3. The larger wins: degree 6.
(c) Which of the following is a polynomial of degree 6?
I. 7x^2II. 4x^6-3x^8III. 2x^3-5x^{-2}+6x^4IV. 4x^2y^4

BIII and IV only

Example 2

Leading coefficient, degree and constant term

The polynomial 4x^3-15+6x^4 has leading coefficient P, degree Q, and constant term R. Find the value of P-Q-R.
Record your answer left to right.

P-Q-R=6-4-(-15)=6-4+15=\mathbf{17}

The course legend: a shaded tile is positive, an unshaded (outline only) tile is negative. One shaded and one unshaded tile of the same kind form a zero pair — together they are worth 0.

Multiplying Binomials

Two binomials make four partial products — First, Outer, Inner, Last. When the outer and inner products are opposites, as in (x+y)(x-y), they cancel and leave a two-term difference of squares.

Find the error: audit every partial product

To check an expansion, verify each partial product separately: coefficients multiply, exponents add, signs multiply.

Example 4

Counting terms, and auditing an expansion

(a) How many terms remain after fully expanding and simplifying (2x-5)(2x+5)?
Record your answer left to right.
4x^2+10x-10x-25=4x^2-25
The middle terms cancel: 2 terms (a difference of squares).
(b) Exactly one of the following expansions is correct. Which one?
A-4x^2(2x^3+3)=-8x^6-12x^2
B-6x(3-2x)=-18x-12x^2
C-3x^3(5+x)=-15x^3-3x^3
D-8x^2(1-x)=-8x^2+8x^3
A: x^{2+3}=x^5 · B: (-6x)(-2x)=+12x^2 · C: (-3x^3)(x)=-3x^4 · D ✓ → D
Think (2x-5)(2x+5) leaves 2 terms. Why does (2x-5)(2x-5) leave 3?
In (2x-5)(2x+5) the outer and inner products are +10x and -10x: they cancel. In (2x-5)^2 both are -10x, so they add: 4x^2-20x+25.

Applying Multiplication to Area

Area of a rectangle = length × width; area of a triangle = \tfrac12 × base × height. With binomial sides, that’s a binomial product. A square with side a+b has area (a+b)^2 — four pieces, not two:

!
The classic error: (a+b)^2\neq a^2+b^2. The two ab rectangles are real area — the middle term is 2ab.
Example 5

Area of a rectangle with binomial sides

The area (in mm²) of a rectangle with length 5-b mm and width 9-b mm is
A45+b^2
B45-4b-b^2
C45-4b+b^2
D45-14b+b^2
(5-b)(9-b)=45-5b-9b+b^2=45-14b+b^2 → D
Example 6

Area of an isosceles right triangle

Triangle PQR is isosceles and right-angled at Q, with legs PQ=QR=2x+6 cm. If the area of the triangle can be written as ax^2+bx+c cm², find the value of b.
A24
B12
C6
D4
The legs are base and height: \tfrac12(2x+6)^2=\tfrac12(4x^2+24x+36)=2x^2+12x+18, so b=12 → B
!
A is a trap: 24 is the middle coefficient of the whole square (2x+6)^2. A right triangle is only half of it.

A shape with a hole: whole − hole

Find the area of the whole shape and the area of the hole, then subtract — with the hole’s area in brackets, so the minus reaches every term.

The Distributive Property with Several Terms

Expand each group separately first, then combine like terms at the very end. A minus in front of a bracket multiplies every term inside by -1.
!
Watch the signs. -4x(x-3)=-4x^2+12x (negative × negative = positive), and -(7x+5)=-7x-5, not -7x+5.
Example 8

Three groups, then collect

The expression 5x(3-2x)-4x(x-3)-(7x+5) can be written in the form ax^2+bx+c. Find the value of abc.
Record your answer left to right.
15x-10x^2-4x^2+12x-7x-5=-14x^2+20x-5
abc=(-14)(20)(-5)=\mathbf{1400}

Multiplying a Binomial by a Trinomial, and Combining Results

Every term of the binomial multiplies every term of the trinomial: 2 × 3 = 6 partial products. On a grid, like terms line up along the diagonals.
Check Example 9(a) by substitution. At t=2: (5\cdot2-2)(3\cdot2^2-4\cdot2-2)=8\times2=16. Then 15(8)-26(4)-2(2)+4 must also be 16.

Extension — Completing the Square

Extension

Completing the square is a Math 20-1 skill and is not part of the Math 10C review.

Every trinomial x^2+bx+c can be rewritten as (x-p)^2+q: take p to be half the coefficient of x, with its sign flipped, then adjust the constant to match.
Example 11

(Extension) Matching coefficients, and completing the square

Use the square-of-a-binomial pattern in reverse.

(a) If (x-a)^2+a=x^2-10x+c, find the value of c.
Record your answer left to right.
x^2-2ax+a^2+a: -2a=-10, so a=5
c=a^2+a=25+5=\mathbf{30}
(b) The trinomial x^2-16x+70 can be expressed in the form (x-p)^2+q. Find the values of p and q.
Half of -16 is -8: (x-8)^2=x^2-16x+64, and 70-64=6
p=8,\ q=6

An Application: Volume of a Folded Box

Cut an x-by-x square from each corner of a sheet, fold up the sides, and you get an open box. Its volume is a product of three factors.

!
Minus 2x, not x. Every side of the sheet loses a square at both ends.
Check at x=2: 4(2)^3-48(2)^2+135(2) should equal 2\times11\times5=110.
Example 12

Cut the corners, fold up the sides

Use the following information. A sheet of paper measures 15 cm by 9 cm. Squares of side x cm (shaded) are cut from each corner, as shown, and the paper is folded along the dashed lines to form an open rectangular box.

(a) The length and width (in cm) of the box are, respectively,
A15-x and 9-x
B15-2x and 9-2x
C15+x and 9+x
D15+2x and 9+2x
A strip x wide comes off both ends of every side: 15-2x and 9-2x → B
(b) The volume of the box can be written as ax^3+bx^2+cx, where a, b and c are integers. Find the value of a+c.
Record your answer left to right.
V=x(15-2x)(9-2x)=x(4x^2-48x+135)
=4x^3-48x^2+135x, so a+c=4+135=\mathbf{139}
Think Which values of x actually make a box?
Every length must be positive: x>0 and 9-2x>0, so 0<x<4.5. (At x=4.5 the width is 0 — no box.)

Common Errors When Expanding Squared Binomials

Order6(3y-2)^2: the square comes first; the 6 multiplies afterwards. Distributing the 6 into the bracket first is wrong.
Square it(3y)^2=9y^2. Squaring the first term is not doubling its coefficient (6y^2).
Middle(mx-n)^2=m^2x^2-2mnx+n^2. Don’t forget the 2 in the middle term.
CheckSubstitute a value into the original and the expansion: they must give the same number.
Example 13(e), left side at y=-3: 6×(3×(−)3−2)x² gives 726.
Right side: bracket the -3 before squaring, or the calculator squares 3 and then negates: 54(-3)^2-72(-3)+24.

Priya, Marcus, and Devon each try to expand 6(3y-2)^2. Their work is shown below. Each student’s work contains exactly one mathematical error.

PriyaMarcusDevon

(b) Describe in detail the error Marcus made.

(c) Describe in detail the error Devon made.
The middle term is 2(3y)(-2)=-12y; he wrote -6y, forgetting the factor 2. His 9y^2 and +4 are right.

Key ideas

Classify: count the terms. Degree = the highest sum of exponents in a term. A negative exponent on a variable means not a polynomial.
Every term × every term: 2 × 2 = 4 and 2 × 3 = 6 partial products, then collect like terms. Exponents add, signs multiply.
Patterns: (a\pm b)^2=a^2\pm2ab+b^2 and (a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2. In k(a+b)^2, square first.
Apply: shaded = whole − (hole); a box from a sheet has V=x(L-2x)(W-2x). Check any expansion by substituting a number.
