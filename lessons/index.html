Using it with the Apple Pencil

Pencil writes, finger scrolls. Rest your palm on the screen while you write.
Tap buttons with either. A Pencil tap on a button presses it instead of writing.
Two-finger tap undoes the last stroke (or use the undo button).
Eraser removes a whole stroke when you touch it. Laser ink fades by itself.
Board opens a blank grid page for questions that come up mid-lesson.
Your ink is saved on this iPad. Clear it from the ••• menu before the next class.
Full screen: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from the icon.
Projecting: Control Centre → Screen Mirroring, same as Notability.
